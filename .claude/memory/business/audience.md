# Audience

Primary: **students** across the K-12 ladder (elementary → middle → high school).
Secondary: **educators** and **parents** who enroll/support students.

## Signals

- Parent testimonial (2019 SpaceSet International Finals): *"I've never seen my
  daughter so engaged and excited to be learning. What an opportunity for her!"*
  — indicates engagement/excitement is a core selling point to parents.
- Programs are segmented by school level, so messaging and product likely differ
  per segment (see `programs.md`).
