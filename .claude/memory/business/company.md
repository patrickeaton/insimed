## Model — how it works

Students participate in **industry simulation competitions and events**: team-based
formats where they tackle real-world industry scenarios, building both technical
and business competencies. Top performers can earn recognition and scholarship
opportunities. Past competition resources are made available to participants.

## Value propositions

- Real-world industry scenarios (not textbook exercises)
- Team-based learning (collaboration, leadership, communication)
- Career aspiration — connects classroom skills to future careers
- Scholarship opportunities
- Access to previous competition work
