# Programs

The offering is a ladder segmented by school level.

| Level             | Offering                    | Status (2026-06-03) |
| ----------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------- |
| High School       | Space Design Competitions   | **Available** — flagship |
| Middle School     | Workshops / team activities | Coming soon |
| Elementary School | Small-group competitions    | Coming soon |

## High School — Space Design Competitions

High-tempo competitions designed to challenge students and prepare them for their
futures. Include scholarship opportunities and access to previous competition work.

## Flagship event

**SpaceSet International Finals** — referenced as far back as 2019. Aligns with the
space-design / STEM theme that runs through the brand.

## Notes

- Middle/Elementary programs are marketed as "coming soon" — roadmap items, not yet
