# Glossary

Domain and methodology terms used across InSim.

## Business / domain

- **InSimEd / Industry Simulation Education** — the company; runs industry
  simulation competitions and events for K-12 students.
- **Industry simulation** — a team-based competition format where students solve
  real-world industry scenarios.
- **Space Design Competition** — the flagship high-school program.
- **SpaceSet International Finals** — the flagship competition event.

## Methodology (used by the skills)

- **Shape Up** — product methodology (pitches, appetite, betting). Source of the
  `create-a-pitch` skill.
- **Pitch** — a shaped piece of work: problem, appetite, solution, rabbit holes,
  no-gos.
- **Appetite** — how much time we're willing to spend; a constraint, not an estimate.
- **MLP (Minimum Lovable Product)** — smallest version people will genuinely love.
- **Tracer bullet / vertical slice** — a thin end-to-end slice through all layers;
  the unit of a `prd-to-plan` phase.

## Tech

- **SST** — infrastructure-as-code framework used for AWS deploys.
- **StaticSite** — SST component for hosting a static SPA.
- **Second brain** — this `.claude/memory/` knowledge base.
